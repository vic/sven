**yen is a query language for Nix configurations.** You write plain Nix data and behavior.
yen answers queries on it: the NixOS, nix-darwin and home-manager systems to build, the errors,
the graph of who gives what to whom, and whether your rules hold.


<CardGrid>
<Card title="Data is queried, not merged" icon="magnifier">

Kinds, instances and typed fields are data. Content in many files never overwrites itself.

</Card>
<Card title="Behavior lands where you say" icon="puzzle">

An aspect is active where its predicate holds. Its elements go to the nearest scope that collects them.

</Card>
<Card title="Questions have answers" icon="approve-check">

Graphs, Datalog with a well-founded semantics, and µKanren with constraints. All pure Nix.

</Card>
<Card title="Safe for agents" icon="warning">

A change is staged, checked against your rules across machines, and refused with its reason. An MCP server answers agents.

</Card>
</CardGrid>
